Classification — Heart Disease Prediction

Binary classification using Random Forest to predict heart disease presence. The model is trained on an 80/20 stratified split with random seed 42.

Performance summary

Key point:

What does this mean? All metrics are above 0.80, indicating the model reliably distinguishes patients with and without heart disease. High recall means the model catches most true disease cases — critical in a screening context where missing a positive case is more costly than a false alarm.

Key point:

What does this mean? The model relies most heavily on chest pain type, maximum heart rate, and ST depression (oldpeak). These align with established cardiology — asymptomatic chest pain and reduced exercise capacity are strong clinical indicators of coronary artery disease.

Diagnostic plots

Key point:

What does this mean? The diagonal cells show correct predictions. Off-diagonal cells show errors — false positives (healthy patients flagged as disease) and false negatives (disease patients missed). In a screening tool, minimising false negatives is the priority.

Key point:

What does this mean? The ROC curve shows how well the model separates disease from no-disease at different thresholds. The further the curve bows towards the top-left corner, the better. An AUC near 0.90 means the model has strong discriminative ability.
