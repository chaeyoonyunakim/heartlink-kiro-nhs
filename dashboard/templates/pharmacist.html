NICE NG136: Severe hypertension

Systolic ≥180 mm Hg or diastolic ≥120 mm Hg. Refer to GP on the same day for assessment and specialist review. Do not wait for ABPM.

Immediate actions

  • Contact the patient's GP practice immediately
  • If the patient has symptoms (headache, visual disturbance, chest pain), call 999
  • Document the reading and referral in the pharmacy clinical record
  • Advise the patient not to drive until reviewed by GP

ABPM Conclusion — Stage 1 Hypertension Confirmed

Daytime ABPM average of 148/92 mm Hg exceeds the Stage 1 threshold (≥135/85) but is below Stage 2 (≥150/95). Night dipping is normal.

GP referral recommendation

  • Refer to GP with this ABPM report within 2 weeks
  • GP to assess 10-year cardiovascular risk (QRISK3)
  • If QRISK3 ≥10%, consider antihypertensive treatment
  • If QRISK3 <10%, offer lifestyle advice and re-check in 12 months

3. NICE NG136 hypertension staging reference

Blood pressure classification thresholds per NICE NG136
Classification Clinic reading ABPM/HBPM daytime average Action
Normal <140/90 mm Hg <135/85 mm Hg Recheck in 5 years
Stage 1 ≥140/90 mm Hg ≥135/85 mm Hg Offer ABPM → GP referral with results
Stage 2 ≥160/100 mm Hg ≥150/95 mm Hg Offer ABPM → urgent GP referral within 1 week
Severe ≥180 systolic or ≥120 diastolic N/A — do not wait for ABPM Same-day GP referral; 999 if symptomatic

Important

  • This is a screening and monitoring tool — it does not replace clinical diagnosis
  • Always refer patients with chest pain or acute symptoms to A&E immediately
  • Eligible patients: aged 40+ without a known hypertension diagnosis
