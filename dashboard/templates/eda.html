Exploratory Data Analysis

Summary statistics, distributions, and correlations across the UCI Heart Disease dataset (920 patients from 4 hospitals).

Correlation heatmap

Key point:

What does this mean? Age and maximum heart rate (thalch) show a moderate negative correlation — older patients tend to achieve lower peak heart rates. Most other features are weakly correlated, suggesting they capture distinct clinical information and are suitable as independent predictors.

Target variable distribution

Key point:

What does this mean? The dataset is roughly balanced between patients with and without heart disease. This means the classifier is not biased towards one class, and accuracy is a meaningful metric.

Key point:

What does this mean? Cholesterol distributions overlap substantially between disease and no-disease groups. Cholesterol alone is not a strong discriminator — the model needs multiple features together to make accurate predictions.

Numerical feature distributions

Histograms show the spread of each continuous variable. Skewed distributions and outliers justify the use of median imputation over mean imputation.
