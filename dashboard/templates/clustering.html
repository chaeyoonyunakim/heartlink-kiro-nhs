Clustering — Patient Risk Group Discovery

Unsupervised KMeans clustering with PCA dimensionality reduction. High-missingness variables (ca, thal, slope) are excluded to prevent hospital bias. Optimal k is selected by silhouette score across k = 2 to 8.

Cluster selection

Important: Clinical interpretation

Cluster assignments are exploratory and should not be used for clinical decision-making without further validation. The clusters reflect data patterns, not confirmed diagnostic categories.
